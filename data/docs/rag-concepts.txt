Retrieval augmented generation, or RAG, gives a language model relevant text found in a document collection so its answer is grounded in sources instead of memory alone. Documents are split into chunks, indexed, and the chunks most similar to a question are retrieved. Smaller chunks are more precise but may lose context, while larger chunks keep context but add noise. BM25 ranks chunks by keyword matches adjusted for word rarity and document length. Embedding search compares meaning using vectors. Hybrid search combines keyword and vector scores. Hallucination is when a model states something unsupported, and citing retrieved chunks makes answers easier to verify.
