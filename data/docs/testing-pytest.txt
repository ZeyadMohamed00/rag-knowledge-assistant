Pytest finds test functions whose names start with test and runs them. A plain assert statement is enough to check a result, and pytest explains failures clearly. Fixtures provide reusable setup, such as a database or a test client, and are passed to tests by argument name. Parametrize runs one test with several inputs. Tests should be independent, fast and deterministic. Mocking replaces a slow or external dependency, such as a network call, with a controlled fake during a test.
